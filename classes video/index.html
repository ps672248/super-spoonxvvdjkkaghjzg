PART 1 OF 3: THE CORE THEOREM
The Harmonic Invariance Law ($A \times B = \text{Constant}$)

Whenever two quantities multiply to maintain a constant product, any fractional surge in $A$ requires an exact harmonic reduction in $B$:

$$\text{If } A \text{ increases by } +\frac{1}{x} \implies B \text{ MUST decrease by } -\frac{1}{x+1}$$

🔑 Key Multipliers

$+25\% (+1/4) \iff -20\% (-1/5)$

$+33.33\% (+1/3) \iff -25\% (-1/4)$

$+16.66\% (+1/6) \iff -14.28\% (-1/7)$

🎯 Product Application

$\text{Price} \times \text{Consumption} = \text{Expenditure}$

$\text{Speed} \times \text{Time} = \text{Distance}$

$\text{Efficiency} \times \text{Days} = \text{Total Work}$

PART 2 OF 3: SPEED-TIME & WORK DYNAMICS
Kinematics & Work Invariance Transfer

The exact same Invariance Law governs Motion and Industrial Work:

$$\text{Speed } \times \text{Time} = \text{Distance} \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Efficiency} \times \text{Days} = \text{Total Work}$$

🚂 Velocity ➔ Time Scaling

Speed drops by $-\frac{1}{x} \implies$ Time surges by $+\frac{1}{x-1}$.

Example: Speed $-1/7 (-14.28\%) \implies$ Time $+1/6 = \mathbf{+16.66\%}$.

⚙️ Efficiency ➔ Days Scaling

Efficiency rises by $+\frac{1}{x} \implies$ Days drop by $-\frac{1}{x+1}$.

Example: Efficiency $+1/3 (+33.33\%) \implies$ Days $-1/4 = \mathbf{-25\%}$.

PART 3 OF 3: SUCCESSIVE COMPOUNDING & TRAPS
The Asymmetry Trap & 2D Area Dilations

Sequential percentage changes compound multiplicatively. A symmetric $+20\%$ followed by $-20\%$ is NEVER $0\%$:

$$\text{Net Multiplier} = \left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)\left(1 - \frac{r}{100}\right) \implies \mathbf{\text{Net Deficit}} = -\left(\frac{r}{10}\right)^2\%$$

📐 2D Geometry Dilations

If side/radius expands by $+k\%$, Area expands by $(1 + k/100)^2 - 1$.

Example: Side $+25\% (5/4) \implies \text{Area } (5/4)^2 = 25/16 = \mathbf{+56.25\%}$.

🏷️ Successive Discounts

Two discounts of $d_1$ and $d_2$ give: $\text{Net} = d_1 + d_2 - \frac{d_1 d_2}{100}$

Example: $20\% + 10\% \implies 30 - 2 = \mathbf{28\% \text{ Single Discount}}$.
