# Quora Marketing — Aspirant Arcade

Manual playbook for finding questions, writing answers, and posting on Quora to drive traffic to [aspirant-arcade.vercel.app](https://aspirant-arcade.vercel.app).

---

## Why Quora

- Quora answers rank on Google for years
- PSU/GATE audience actively searches here
- One good answer = free organic traffic indefinitely
- No bot needed — manual quality > automated spam

---

## Step 1 — Find Questions

### Search inside Quora
Go to [quora.com](https://quora.com) → search bar → try these:

```
PSU interview preparation
how to prepare for GATE PSU
BHEL NTPC ONGC interview tips
PSU GD PI round preparation
best app for PSU exam
how to crack PSU selection process
engineering govt job India
GATE PSU jobs 2025 2026
IOCL PGCIL HAL interview
PSU technical interview tips
```

### Search via Google (finds high-traffic questions)
Paste these into Google:

```
site:quora.com PSU interview preparation India
site:quora.com GATE PSU jobs how to prepare
site:quora.com BHEL NTPC ONGC interview tips
site:quora.com best app PSU exam preparation
site:quora.com how to crack PSU selection process
site:quora.com engineering government job India preparation
```

### Pick questions with:
- 10+ followers on the question
- 5+ existing answers (means traffic exists)
- Asked recently OR asked long ago with no good answer (opportunity)

---

## Step 2 — Write the Answer

### Structure (always follow this)

```
[Hook — address their exact question directly, 1-2 lines]

[Helpful body — 3-5 practical points, no fluff]

[App mention — natural, at end, 1-2 lines max]

[Link]
```

### Rules
- Answer the question FIRST — be genuinely useful
- Mention Aspirant Arcade only once, at the end
- Don't say "I made this app" — say "I found/used this app"
- Max 1 link per answer
- Hinglish is fine if question is in Hindi/Hinglish
- No copy-paste — Quora detects duplicate content and collapses answers

### App mention template (vary these, don't repeat exact wording)

```
For GD and PI mock practice, I used Aspirant Arcade (aspirant-arcade.vercel.app) —
free web app with AI group discussion simulator and technical + HR PI mock rounds.
No signup needed.
```

```
There's a free tool called Aspirant Arcade that has gamified MCQ practice
filtered by PSU (BHEL, ONGC, NTPC etc.) and AI mock interview rounds.
Worth trying: aspirant-arcade.vercel.app
```

```
Aspirant Arcade pe try karo — free hai, signup nahi chahiye, aur PSU-specific
MCQ aur AI mock interview dono available hain. aspirant-arcade.vercel.app
```

## Step 4 — Posting Original Questions (Self-Answer)

You can ask a question yourself and answer it. Works well for:

```
"What is the best free tool for PSU interview preparation in 2026?"
"Which app is best for practicing PSU technical interview?"
"Is Aspirant Arcade good for GATE PSU preparation?"
```

Steps:
1. Ask the question (logged in)
2. Wait 10 minutes
3. Answer your own question thoroughly
4. Quora will index it — it will rank on Google over time

## Daily Routine (30 min/day)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 10 min | Find 2-3 new questions via Google `site:quora.com` search |
| 15 min | Write 2 quality answers |
| 5 min | Post 1 original question (self-answer) |

Target: **2 answers/day minimum** = 60 answers/month = significant SEO footprint

---

## What NOT to Do

- Don't paste same answer on multiple questions — Quora collapses duplicates
- Don't put link in every answer — space it out (every 2nd or 3rd answer)
- Don't answer with just app promotion — answer first, promote second
- Don't create multiple accounts — Quora bans for this
- Don't answer irrelevant questions just to drop link
